# GS308 Gigabit Ethernet Switch.

The bracket to hold the GS308 will have a single 
shelf with fins to support the unit on the side.
and some way to stop the unit from sliding back 
when the front is pushed. 

The front should have an aperture with a 1mm 
lip all around such that the unit cannot fall out
on the front. 

With calipers I measure the front of the SG308 to
be 26mm tall and my ruler says the front is 153 mm wide.
It is 101mm deep. There are rubber button feet that 
extend below the box, but these can be removed.

On both sides, from 45mm to 90mm measured from 
the front, vertically centered and 12mm tall,
there are ventilation holes.
