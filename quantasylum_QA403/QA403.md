# QuantAsylum QA403 Audio Analyzer

From the QuantAsylum product page
(https://quantasylum.com/products/qa403-audio-analyzer):
"177mm wide x 44 tall x 97 deep (115 mm including BNC connectors)."


The BNC face (balanced inputs/outputs, status LEDs) goes to the front, so the
unit is 177 mm wide in the rack. At 44 mm it is taller than a 1U panel
(43.65 mm), so the bracket is 2U.

The bracket holds the unit on a single shelf with side fins and a low rear
lip, like the GS308 bracket. The front aperture has a 2 mm lip all round so
the unit cannot fall out on the front; the BNCs stick out through it.

There is a vent on each side, 12 mm tall and 40 mm long, located 30 mm from the
back and 25 mm from the bottom of the 44 mm tall box (the feet are below the
44 mm tall box). The fins have a window there, 1.5 mm bigger than the vent all
round.

The Rubber feet are 13mm in diameter, round, and 6mm tall. The feet are 151mm
apart side-to-side and 77mm apart front-to-back (the feet layout is centered 
on the bottom). I would like tracks in the
bottom of the shelf such that the feet sit in the track.

## Feet tracks

The shelf has two tracks running front-to-back at ±75.5 mm from the centre,
15 mm wide (the feet plus 1 mm each side, more than the side-wall clearance so
the fins locate the unit, not the feet) and 6 mm deep, the height of the feet.
The body then rests on the shelf with the feet just reaching the track floor.
The tracks are deeper than the 4 mm shelf, so each is a 3 mm floor, 2 mm wall
channel hanging under the shelf, closed by the panel at the front and the rear
lip at the back; the channels also stiffen the shelf. The feet can be at any
front-to-back position along the tracks, so the 77 mm spacing only matters for
the `show_unit` ghost.
