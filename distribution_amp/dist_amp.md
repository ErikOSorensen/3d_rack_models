# BNC distribution amplifier

A distribution amplifier with its BNC outputs on the front and a single
input BNC on the back.

- The case is 165 mm wide, 105 mm deep and 31 mm tall.
- 1U bracket, the unit centred on the panel. The aperture is 3 mm smaller
  than the case all around, so the panel holds the unit at the front.
- Shelf with solid side fins. A 5 mm high lip across the back of the shelf
  stops the unit sliding back when cables are pushed onto the front BNCs; it
  is low enough to stay clear of the input BNC on the back.
- The unit is dropped in from above and lifted straight out.

`dist_amp_bracket.scad` holds the numbers; the geometry is `shelf_bracket()`
in `../lib/rack10.scad`.
